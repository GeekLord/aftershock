Find the worst first, with evidence. Built on Meta Muse Spark, SAM 3.1 and Voice Transcribe.
Pick a sample or upload a drone image or video.
Ranked areas appear here.
What this is not. A triage aid that ranks where to look first; not a structural assessment or a rescue decision. Measured zero-shot on held-out drone imagery: severe-damage recall 90% and precision 76% (RescueNet, n=200); exact four-class grade 66%, within one grade 95.5%. Flooded-road detection 88% recall, 100% precision (FloodNet, n=60). SAM misses flattened buildings, so area scans cover them. ⚑ marks anything a person must check: assessors disagree, the scan and grader conflict, or the scene is too dense. Every head-count from radio must be read back.
